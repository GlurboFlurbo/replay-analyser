Double jumps

An air jump (the second jump); the first jump from the ground is not counted. Jumps after simply walking off a ledge are. On maps that allow extra jumps, every air jump counts. Per 10 min uses the time each player was visible in the recording.

Emotes

Every emote bubble the recording client received, by type. Players whose character was never in view can still appear here, because emotes are broadcast to the whole server. Click a column header to sort by that emote. With default DDNet spam protection, emotes repeated within three seconds are only sent to nearby players, so far-away spam can be missing.
